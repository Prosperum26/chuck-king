🎮 About Us

We are a team of 9 developers united by a passion for building challenging and expressive gameplay systems.

Chuck King began as an experimental idea and evolved into a full collaborative production — combining gameplay engineering, AI systems, narrative design, art, sound, and testing into one cohesive experience.

We don't just build games.
We build controlled chaos.

🎯 Our Vision

Chuck King is designed around tension, precision, and consequence.

Every jump matters.
Every fall hurts.
Every victory feels earned.

Our goal was to create a physics-based rage platformer that challenges both skill and patience, while experimenting with AI-driven interaction and expressive feedback systems.
